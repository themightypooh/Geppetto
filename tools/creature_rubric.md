# Creature judging rubric — FORM pass (clay renders, no materials)

Score each criterion 0–10. The ONLY passing bar is 9+ (would sit next to the reference artists); there is no "keepable".
Be harsh and specific. Reference bar: Jef Wall's Still Wakes the Deep sculpts, Rob Bottin's
The Thing, Sebastien Giroux's Dead Space Wheezer. The sheets are in
~/.claude/skills/organic-modeling/references/swtd/ — LOOK at them before scoring. Judge the
sculpt only: colour is deliberately absent.

1. **Silhouette** — one clear gesture from front, side and 3/4; still reads at thumbnail;
   limbs separate from mass; no visual mush; the black silhouettes are the test.
2. **Wrong human** — identifiable human anatomy (faces, hands, boots, trousers, ribcage,
   spine) at the right scale, intact enough to recognise a *person*, placed where it hurts.
   Faces must read as faces in clay.
3. **Grotesque craft** — fusion looks grown, not glued; gut coils, tendrils, lumps, pustules,
   drips, teeth convincing at body scale; surface has lumps, folds and striations, not smooth
   balloons; nothing reads as noodles, sausages or toys.
4. **Design hierarchy** — one primary mass, secondary limbs/eruptions, tertiary detail; a focal
   point; deliberate asymmetry; scale contrast (thin against huge); big/medium/small sizes.
5. **Weight & grounding** — mass feels heavy; legs plant; floor contact reads; nothing floats;
   no interpenetration that looks like a modelling error.
6. **Memorability** — would a player remember and describe it; does ONE idea land.
7. **Production** — clean enough to rig and animate; parts separable (limbs, head, tendrils);
   not a clump.

Report per creature: the 7 scores, an overall (your gut, not the mean), the single worst
problem, and 5 concrete fixes phrased as geometry changes (what, where, how big — use the
creature's own part names and inches; the creatures are 60–100 in across). Then rank the
three and say which has the highest ceiling.
