# Head judging rubric — distorted human heads for body horror (clay)

Score each criterion 0–10. The ONLY passing bar is 9+: would pass next to Jef Wall's Still Wakes the
Deep heads (~/.claude/skills/organic-modeling/references/swtd/ — look at the faces first).
These heads get fused into creature flesh at the neck; judge everything above the neck cut.

1. **Reads as a real person** — a believable human face, not a mannequin or a cartoon. Do NOT
   ask for age, wrinkles, weight or a different build: the base face is fixed by the owner.
2. **Distortion is anatomical** — when the jaw tears, the face smears or eyes multiply, it
   looks like flesh and bone forced past their limits (skin stretching, folds bunching, bone
   showing, lips splitting), not like a mesh dragged with a soft brush.
3. **Mouth & teeth** — teeth read as real teeth in a real mouth; gums, tongue, cavity depth;
   the opening has lips with thickness, corners that tear; nothing floating or broken.
4. **Eyes** — eyes read as eyes (lids, sockets, the gaze), extra eyes look grown into sockets,
   nothing is a hole or a ball stuck on.
5. **Expression / horror beat** — does the face communicate agony, terror, vacancy; would a
   player flinch.
6. **Cleanliness** — no artefacts: ragged cuts, flaps, holes, interpenetration, broken shells.
7. **Variety** — the four read as four different men and four different fates.

Report per head (crewman, howl, smear, droop): the 7 scores, an overall, the worst problem, and
5 concrete fixes phrased as geometry operations in the head's own inches (origin skull centre,
x forward, z up; chin at z −4.7, lips −3.1, nose tip x 5.0, eyes z +0.4 at y ±1.25, crown +4.8),
e.g. "pull the lower lip down 1.5 in centred at (4.3, 0, −3.4) radius 1.2". Then the single
change that would lift all four the most.
